# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: February 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to **Rhythm**, an offline music player application developed by **Nousher Murtaza** ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent user experience. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and the permissions required for the application's functionality.

## 2. Information Collection and Usage
Rhythm is designed as a focused, offline-first utility.
- **Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations.
- **Usage Data**: We do not employ any cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services to monitor your interaction with the application.
- **No Data Sharing**: Since no data is collected, we do not share, sell, or trade any user information with third parties.

## 3. Required Permissions and Data Access
To provide its intended functionality, Rhythm requires access to specific device features. All data accessed through these permissions remains local to your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

- **Media and Storage Access**: Needed to scan and index audio files stored on your device for playback. 
- **Wake Lock**: Ensures uninterrupted audio playback when the device screen is off.
- **Foreground Service**: Allows the application to maintain playback and display media controls in the system notification panel while running in the background.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Rhythm does not target children under the age of 13, nor do we knowingly collect personal information from them. Our application is safe for all audiences and complies with standard privacy protections for minors.

## 5. Security
We take the security of your device and the integrity of the application seriously. By operating primarily offline and requiring minimal permissions, Rhythm minimizes potential security risks associated with data transmission.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy as necessary to reflect changes in our practices or for legal and regulatory reasons. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
